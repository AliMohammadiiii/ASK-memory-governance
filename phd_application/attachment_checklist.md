## Formal PhD application

The current course directory lists the following items. See [PhD in Computer Science - How to Apply](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/directory/cscspdpcs/apply).

- [ ] Two referees confirmed and briefed; references submitted by the relevant deadline.
- [ ] Transcript uploaded.
- [ ] CV/resume uploaded.
- [ ] Evidence of competence in English uploaded if required.
- [ ] Research proposal uploaded; final body remains at or below 3,000 words.
- [ ] One sample of work selected and uploaded.
- [ ] Reasons for Applying response drafted within 1,500 characters.
- [ ] Multiple Proposed Supervisors response drafted within 200 characters.
- [ ] School of Technology widening-participation/contextual questionnaire completed if still required.
- [ ] Application fee status checked and fee paid if applicable.
- [ ] Applicant Portal submission completed.
- [ ] Referee submission status checked; the application is incomplete until required references arrive.

## Supervisor-permission gate

- [ ] Proposed research discussed with the prospective supervisor.
- [ ] Explicit permission received to name that person on the application.
- [ ] Confirmation received that the research can be undertaken in the Department.
- [ ] Confirmation received that the prospective supervisor can accept new PhD students.

Until all four items are satisfied, use **prospective supervisor** in private planning documents and do not represent an agreement.

## Academic and English evidence

- [ ] Degree certificates and transcripts show final or expected award dates accurately.
- [ ] GPA values and any class-rank statements match official records.
- [ ] International-qualification equivalence checked for Iran.
- [ ] English evidence checked against the course-specific thresholds: IELTS Academic 7.5 overall with 7.0 in each element, TOEFL iBT 107 overall with 25 in each element, or the stated Cambridge English alternatives. See [course requirements](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/directory/cscspdpcs/requirements).
- [ ] A self-assessed CEFR level or a CV statement such as "English B2" is not treated as Cambridge test evidence.
