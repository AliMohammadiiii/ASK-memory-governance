\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

We position \ask{} against five clusters. For each we name the nearest precedent
and state precisely what we build on. Our claim concerns the operationalization
and controlled evaluation of a particular combination, not priority for any
individual component.

\paragraph{Agent memory systems with write operations.}
MemGPT/Letta \citep{packer2023memgpt}, Mem0 \citep{chhikara2025mem0}, A-MEM
\citep{xu2025amem}, and RMM \citep{tan2025rmm} manage a persistent store with
per-item operations (add/update/delete/no-op, or merge-vs-add). Memory-R1
\citep{yan2025memoryr1} is the clearest prior formalization of memory writes as a
discrete action space, learned by RL. We do not claim to introduce the action
space. The differences we defend are that (i) \texttt{NOOP} in these systems means
``nothing new,'' not ``too ambiguous to commit,'' so there is no
uncertainty-driven refusal; and (ii) they are primarily rewarded at the answer
level, without a shared diagnostic that separately scores the write and its
resulting state. Our released rule is a transparent reference policy, not a
claim of a new learning algorithm.

\paragraph{Write-time conflict resolution and lineage.}
Zep/Graphiti \citep{rasmussen2025zep} invalidates contradicted graph edges with
bi-temporal timestamps rather than deleting them --- the strongest shipped prior
on supersession-with-history. TOKI \citep{wang2026toki} argues, formally, that
contradiction resolution \emph{is} write-time concurrency control, and types the
heuristic family (including an \emph{await-confirmation} operator functionally
equivalent to \abstain) as bitemporal operators with audit rows. FadeMem
\citep{fademem2026} classifies new-vs-old memory pairs and applies decay/fusion.
Relative to these, storing lineage or provenance is not new; our distinct piece is
to make the write a \emph{governed, scored} decision with an abstain option, and to
measure lineage integrity separately from the action label. \citet{reddy2026deterministic}
independently report that deterministic conflict resolution beats LLM judgment on a
consolidation benchmark, motivating independent tests of transparent policies rather
than validating our author-designed pilot by analogy.

\paragraph{Typed memory.}
MemGuard \citep{ha2026memguard} assigns each memory an explicit functional role at
write time to prevent contamination, and MemOS \citep{li2025memos} types memory
levels. Typed memory is therefore precedented; what we add is a measured ablation
of typing inside the pilot, reported as a mechanism diagnostic rather than an
independently identified causal effect.

\paragraph{Diagnostics and evaluation for memory.}
HaluMem \citep{halumem2025} is the nearest prior on operation-level evaluation,
separately scoring extraction, update, and QA for hallucinations; we do not claim
to introduce operation-level evaluation. STALE \citep{chao2026stale} probes
\emph{frozen agents at read time} for recognition of stale beliefs and shares
\mgp's construct names (implicit conflict, premise resistance), but defines no
write actions, abstention, or lineage. The Supersede environment
\citep{patel2026supersede} isolates the answer-level ``supersession gap'' on
\lme{} and trains the behavior end-to-end. GateMem \citep{ren2026gatemem} uses the
phrase ``memory governance'' for authorization across principals --- a different
axis from our epistemic write correctness. LongMemEval \citep{wu2025longmemeval}
and LoCoMo \citep{maharana2024locomo} are end-to-end QA benchmarks that cannot
attribute a failure to the write decision. Newer evaluations close other parts
of that gap: Memora \citep{uddin2026memora} penalizes obsolete-memory use;
MemGym \citep{xu2026memgym} reports memory-isolated scores across agentic
regimes; and GroupMemBench \citep{yang2026groupmembench} adds multi-principal
knowledge-update and abstention cases. None supplies the same controlled
three-way write label together with a post-transition lineage target. \mgp{}
operationalizes joint scoring of write-time \keep/\supersede/\abstain{}
decisions and resulting memory state; we do not claim that operation-level,
memory-isolated, or state-level evaluation itself is new.

\paragraph{Selective prediction, belief revision, knowledge editing.}
The reject option \citep{chow1970reject,elyaniv2010selective,geifman2017selective}
and learning to defer \citep{madras2018defer,mozannar2020defer} are the origin of
\abstain; RSCB-MC \citep{iscan2026rscbmc} already applies abstention to memory
\emph{retrieval} in coding agents. We therefore never claim to introduce
abstention to memory systems; we measure what an explicit abstain-on-write option
contributes within our controlled diagnostic and claim no priority for the
mechanism. RSCB-MC is also the closest feature-controller precedent: its
16-feature contextual bandit governs whether retrieved coding memory should be
injected, whereas our five-feature rule governs a candidate write and exposes
the exact score and state transition. The distinction is scope and
auditability, not ownership of feature-based control. AGM belief revision
\citep{alchourron1985agm} is the intellectual
ancestor of \keep/\supersede; we cite it as ancestry only and prove no
postulate compliance. Knowledge editing \citep{meng2022rome,meng2023memit}
governs \emph{how} to change a parametric belief and assumes the edit should
happen; we govern \emph{whether} to commit an external write. Finally, PrefEval
\citep{zhao2025prefeval} and PersonaMem \citep{jiang2025personamem} motivate
testing preference changes on independently constructed natural dialogue.

\paragraph{Scope of contribution.}
Each component has named precedents: Memory-R1 for write actions; Zep and TOKI
for retained history; MemGuard and MemOS for typing; deterministic policy work
including \citet{reddy2026deterministic}; and HaluMem and STALE for evaluation.
We therefore make the
narrower contribution claim that this work operationalizes and audits an explicit
three-way policy --- \keep, \supersede, or \abstain{} --- over typed,
provenance-tracked
records with supersession lineage, and evaluates that implementation at three
separate layers: decision, stored state/lineage, and downstream answer. The
controlled conjunction, its validity checks, and the measurements they enable
are the contribution;
update operations, version history, provenance, typing, abstention, and
operation-level evaluation are not claimed as inventions.
