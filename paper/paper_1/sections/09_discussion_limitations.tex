\section{Discussion, Threats to Validity, and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:limitations}

\subsection{What the evidence supports}

Four claims are supported, at different levels. First, separating write, state,
and answer failures is diagnostically useful: the v2 no-lineage ablation leaves
action accuracy unchanged at $0.9222$ while lineage integrity falls to
$0.3689$, a corruption invisible to a decision-only score. Second, the
five-dimensional scorecard is the actual \lme{} runtime policy, not a paper
abstraction: exact replay recovers every action, and counterfactual replay shows
that $f$, $r$, and the relation guard carry nearly all action variation on this
run (Section~\ref{sec:downstream:scorecard}). Third, on \mgpc{} the composed
controller is far above chance on a balanced decision where the evaluated
baselines, adapter-mapped external systems, and ablations are not, and neither
controller component alone explains the development result
(Sections~\ref{sec:results:dev}--\ref{sec:results:guard}). Fourth, on the
homogeneous \lme{} slice, governed writes exceed every structural baseline
($76.1\%$ versus $\le 63.3\%$, paired $p=2.9\times10^{-4}$), with the largest
gain in knowledge update.

Five stronger readings are not supported. (i) No \mgpc{} hidden-split system
comparison exists. Only one configuration was authorised, so every baseline,
ablation, external-system, successor, and component contrast is development
evidence on the split used for selection. (ii) Neither diagnostic estimates a
deployment population. Cluster intervals quantify uncertainty within the
constructed \mgpc{} collection, and the \lme{} result covers one stratified
$180$-item slice. (iii) A $0.0444$ state-corrupting false-commit rate on
synthetic data is not a safety certification; its upper confidence bound is
$0.0852$. (iv) The \lme{} action log has no per-candidate gold write label, so
exact scorecard replay and leave-one-feature-out changes establish mechanism,
not action correctness, threshold optimality, or a causal benefit of the five
features. The v4.3 scorecard adapter's $0.3333$ development accuracy is direct
evidence against making that stronger claim. (v) Monetary cost is unavailable:
we report measured tokens and latency rather than imputed billing.

One defect is also substantive. The frozen \mgpc{} configuration's selective
accuracy ($0.8449$) is below its overall accuracy ($0.8481$), so abstentions are
not preferentially placed on items it would otherwise get wrong. The
abstention mechanism performs governance work without functioning as a
calibrated selective predictor; these are different claims.

\subsection{Threats to validity}

\paragraph{Internal and construct validity.}
The v2 pilot fails directly: the same project designed the taxonomy, generator,
gold, and reference policy, and conflict type perfectly predicts the held-out
action. Family-cluster bootstrap cannot repair a deterministic
family--label map. The v4.3 redesign closes that measured shortcut through
surface-matched action variants, component-disjoint splits, restricted-view
probes, and duplicate gates, but it remains author-designed and stylized.
Passing the implemented probes means those probes did not recover the action;
it does not prove that no untested shortcut exists.

The scorecard thresholds and weights are hand-specified configuration, not
learned or independently calibrated. On V12 the stability prior changes no
action and common thresholds change only five. Type conditioning is therefore
implemented but weakly exercised, and the observed type-specific action rates
must not be read as validation of the chosen durability ordering.

\paragraph{External validity.}
\mgpc{} is synthetic, English-only, single-slot, and single-step. It lacks
natural conversational trajectories, interference between slots, multiple
languages, and a population sampling frame. The \lme{} run supplies naturalistic
long-horizon QA structure but remains one fixed slice, answer model, judge, and
seed. The two instruments complement rather than validate each other:
\mgpc{} has write labels without realism; \lme{} has downstream realism without
per-write labels.

\paragraph{Annotation.}
The instruments have different evidence and must not be conflated. V2 has one
independent human re-annotation of its $90$-item test split plus a separate
four-model audit panel; that is not multi-human agreement. V4.3 has two
completed $900$-item packets with perfect action agreement and evidence-turn
citations, but completion and reviewer identity are owner-attested. Perfect
agreement on a deterministic manual establishes consistency, not independent
construct validity.

\paragraph{Statistical conclusion.}
The v2 test contains only $48$ template families; its reference--LLM interval is
a null, not evidence of equality. On \mgpc{} the hidden component-cluster
interval is materially wider than the item-independence Wilson interval and is
the one we privilege. Development intervals are item bootstraps because the
public scorer does not read the private component map; the paired McNemar tests
therefore ignore within-component dependence. Their largest effects are
descriptively unambiguous, but the $0.117$ successor contrast is especially
sensitive to that assumption. On \lme{}, only the aggregate governed-versus-
naive-overwrite test supports inference after correction; the competency cells
are descriptive at $n=30$ each.

\paragraph{Selection and one-shot protocol.}
The \mgpc{} hidden result is one measurement of one configuration. Development
selection is the protocol working as intended, but the successor's $0.961$
development score is not evidence that it improves the frozen controller's
$0.848$ hidden score. For \lme{}, the homogeneous V12 run supersedes the
contaminated v7 and selection-biased replay constructions; those earlier
artifacts remain in the paper only to document why they were rejected.

\paragraph{Operational provenance.}
The v4.3 hidden run resumed once after a transport disconnect under an immutable
manifest, with complete coverage and no duplicate prediction. V12 has two open
bookkeeping issues --- a stale launcher code/protocol hash and a manifest not
written to a terminal status after resume --- while its per-system answer,
judge-routing, and fallback checks pass. These do not change the reported
predictions, but they prevent a stronger clean-room provenance claim.
Provider-reported latency is route-dependent wall time, not hardware compute.
The V12 answer pipeline contains a deterministic computation aid in addition
to the write controller. It produced no direct model bypass and its two
controller-side answer substitutions had zero net accuracy effect, so it does
not explain the reported controller--naive contrast; we nevertheless disclose
it because downstream accuracy is not a write-only measurement.

\subsection{Ethics and scope}

Both diagnostics contain synthetic, low-risk scenarios and no real personal
data. Our target is epistemic write correctness. Memory poisoning,
multi-principal authorization, privacy leakage, deletion compliance, and
security governance are separate threat models that we do not test. We make no
production-safety claim.

\subsection{Successor-dataset gate}

A stronger release must be constructed after policies and thresholds are
frozen; use independent generation and hidden labels; include \keep,
\supersede, and \abstain{} within every conflict family; add adversarial minimal
pairs and 5/10/20-step trajectories; obtain blinded labels from at least three
humans; and include natural or independently paraphrased dialogue. Hidden
baselines and ablations must be authorised before any hidden scoring. These are
requirements for stronger claims, not future-work decorations.

\subsection{Reproducibility statement}

The repository releases schemas, validators, policies, public data, per-item
predictions, and the V12 action log. The analysis programs regenerate shortcut
tables, cluster intervals, calibration, risk--coverage, cost sensitivity, and
the five-dimensional scorecard replay. Every scorecard row carries enough state
to recompute both scores and the final action without a model call. Incomplete,
mapped, contaminated, and selection-biased artifacts retain their exact status
instead of being silently averaged into primary results. Reproduction commands
are in Appendix~\ref{app:repro}.

\subsection{v4.3 scope and release limitations}

MGP-6 v4.3 is a controlled synthetic diagnostic, not evidence of real-world
generalization or deployment safety. Its private hidden gold, final human gold,
mappings, completed annotations, consensus, and detailed audits remain outside
version control and model-facing inputs. No public redistribution license has
been selected, so the data should not be publicly hosted until licensing is
resolved; aggregate results and cryptographic commitments do not remove that
restriction.
