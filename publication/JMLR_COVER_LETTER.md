# JMLR cover-letter working template

Status: `BLOCKED_HUMAN_DECISION`

1. Manuscript title and corresponding author.
2. Originality statement and disclosure of every overlapping preprint,
   workshop, conference, journal, thesis, or simultaneous submission; explain
   the substantive delta.
3. Confirmation that every co-author knows of and consents to submission.
4. Funding and conflict declaration, including recently collaborating Action
   Editors.
5. Three to five suitable, non-conflicted JMLR Action Editors with rationale.
6. Three to five suitable, non-conflicted reviewers with rationale.
7. Five keywords.
8. If the paper exceeds 50 pages including appendices, justification.
9. Artifact/data/code availability and any redistribution restrictions.

No names may be filled automatically from repository metadata.
