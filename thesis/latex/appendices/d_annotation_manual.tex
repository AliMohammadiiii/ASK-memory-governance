The annotation manual is
\texttt{benchmarks/mgp6/annotation\_manual\_v2.md}; the kit builder is
\texttt{make\_annotation\_kit.py}; agreement computation is
\texttt{compute\_agreement\_v2.py}; raw annotation files live under
\texttt{benchmarks/mgp6/annotations/human/} (independent annotator) and
\texttt{mgp6\_v2/annotation/} (four-LLM panel). The single open
adjudication item is \texttt{MGP6V2-T3-TEST-016} (gold SUPERSEDE vs
annotator ABSTAIN, implicit conflict). Adjudication protocol: blinded
display of the raw item and both labels; evidence spans and policy
principle for each; resolution \ensuremath{\in} \{released, alternative, both
defensible, underspecified\}; logged with adjudicators, date, and
version impact. Main results use released gold; sensitivity may score
the alternative label or exclude the item (±1 item = 1.1\% of the test
split).
