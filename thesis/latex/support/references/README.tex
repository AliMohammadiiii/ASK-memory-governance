\section{Current checks}\label{current-checks}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Citation-key completeness is checked mechanically: every
  \texttt{{[}@key{]}} in the thesis must have a matching BibTeX key.
\item
  Duplicate BibTeX keys are prohibited.
\item
  The bibliography is not yet formatted to an official University of
  Tehran style.
\item
  Entries whose complete author metadata was not verified retain an
  explicit \texttt{note} rather than invented details.
\end{itemize}

\section{Human verification still
required}\label{human-verification-still-required}

Before institutional submission, verify each cited work against its
primary publication page or official proceedings record:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  complete author order and spelling;
\item
  exact title and capitalization;
\item
  venue, volume, issue, pages, and year;
\item
  DOI, anthology identifier, or stable URL;
\item
  preprint-to-published-version replacement where applicable; and
\item
  consistency with the University's required bibliography style.
\end{enumerate}

The thesis's novelty boundary does not depend on a primacy claim. The
related-work section positions ASK as operationalizing and evaluating a
combined write-policy and layered-measurement design.
