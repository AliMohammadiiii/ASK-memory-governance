\section{9.7 Ethics, privacy, and dual
use}\label{ethics-privacy-and-dual-use}

A system that supersedes and audits memories can be abused to rewrite
records or manufacture provenance; a system that retains them enables
surveillance, extraction, and membership inference
\citep{carlini2021extracting, shokri2017membership, bender2021dangers, weidinger2022taxonomy}.
Mitigations in this project's scope: lineage is append-only and
superseded records are preserved rather than destroyed; ABSTAIN routes
ambiguity to review instead of committing; the evaluation data is
synthetic (MGP-6) or externally licensed and not redistributed
(LongMemEval); no personal data of real users is stored. Deployment
beyond research requires access control, dual control for bulk
operations, and organizational governance consonant with risk-management
frameworks \citep{nist2023airmf}; alignment techniques at the model
layer \citep{ouyang2022training, bai2022constitutional} complement but
do not replace the deterministic write boundary. The governance
controller is an engineering framework, not legal advice; ``supersede''
and ``abstain'' here are epistemic operations, not compliance
guarantees.

\section{9.8 Reproducibility statement}\label{reproducibility-statement}

Released: code, MGP-6 v2 data, per-item predictions, the 10,000-sample
construct-validity audit, cluster intervals, cost sensitivity,
bibliographic checks, and the v3 confirmation protocol. The v4.3 final
configuration, freeze authorization, aggregate hidden metrics, and
prediction hash are reproducibly recorded, while hidden records and gold
remain private. The few-shot run is incomplete; mapped external systems
and all LongMemEval evidence are non-primary. Independent v3
construction and three-human annotation remain unexecuted. Documentation
follows datasheet/model-card practice
\citep{gebru2021datasheets, mitchell2019modelcards}.
