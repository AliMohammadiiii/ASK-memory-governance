\section{Novelty boundary}\label{novelty-boundary}

The defensible contribution is a layered evaluation decomposition, a
reproducible construct-validity audit, and a transparent successor
protocol. The thesis makes no priority claim for update operations,
abstention, typing, provenance, lineage, governance, or operation-level
evaluation.
